# EDUCORE AI

**EduCore AI** is a comprehensive AI-powered cloud-based school management and learning platform designed specifically for Nigerian primary and secondary schools. It automates the most time-consuming and error-prone aspects of school administration and teaching — from intelligent lesson plan generation and AI-driven exam question creation to automated grading, result computation, and parent communication — all built around the realities of the Nigerian education system: NERDC curriculum alignment, offline-first design, WAEC/NECO/JAMB preparation, and intermittent connectivity.

---

## 🎯 Key Features

### **AI Lesson Plan Generation**
- **NERDC-Aligned Lesson Notes** — Auto-generate complete, structured lesson plans aligned to the official Nigerian curriculum for all subjects and class levels (Primary 1–6, JSS1–3, SS1–3)
- **Bloom's Taxonomy Integration** — Plans are scaffolded across knowledge, comprehension, application, analysis, synthesis, and evaluation levels
- **Teaching Aids & Resources** — Auto-suggest relevant teaching aids, activities, and instructional materials for each topic
- **Editable Templates** — All generated lesson plans are fully editable and can be saved, reused, or shared with other teachers
- **Scheme of Work Generation** — Automatically generate term-long schemes of work from a single subject and class selection
- **Cross-Subject Linking** — Identify and highlight relationships between topics across subjects for interdisciplinary teaching
- **Offline Plan Access** — Download and access lesson plans without internet connectivity

### **AI Exam & Test Question Generation**
- **Multi-Format Question Types** — Generate Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs), Theory, Short Answer, True/False, Fill-in-the-Blank, and Essay questions
- **Curriculum-Mapped Questions** — Every question is mapped to a specific NERDC topic, class level, and term
- **WAEC / NECO / JAMB Pattern Questions** — Generate exam questions modelled after past external examination patterns to prepare students effectively
- **Difficulty Level Control** — Specify Easy, Medium, or Hard difficulty per question or per paper
- **Randomized Exam Papers** — Auto-shuffle questions and options to generate unique papers per student, reducing examination malpractice
- **Question Bank** — Build and grow a school-wide question bank by subject, topic, class, and difficulty — fully searchable and reusable
- **Bulk Paper Generation** — Generate complete examination papers for an entire class or year group in one click
- **Continuous Assessment (CA) Generator** — Quickly produce CA tests per week or topic, reducing teacher workload each term

### **AI Grading & Assessment**
- **Instant Objective Grading** — Auto-grade MCQ and objective tests immediately upon submission with 100% accuracy
- **Structured Answer Scoring** — Grade short-answer and fill-in-the-blank questions using keyword matching and semantic analysis
- **Essay & Long-Form Feedback** — AI evaluates essays against provided rubrics, checks argument quality, grammar, and structure, and provides written feedback
- **Rubric-Based Grading** — Teachers define custom rubrics; AI applies them consistently across all student submissions
- **Continuous Assessment Computation** — Automatically calculate CA scores, test averages, and cumulative term scores per student
- **Result Computation Engine** — Compute final term results, class rankings, grade averages, and subject pass rates automatically
- **WAEC / NECO Score Formatting** — Generate results formatted for external examination registration and internal mock grading
- **Grading Consistency** — Eliminate inter-marker variance with AI-standardized scoring across all papers

### **Student Management**
- **Student Profiles** — Maintain detailed records including biodata, class history, subject enrollment, health notes, and parent contacts
- **Academic History Tracking** — Full record of every student's performance across terms and years
- **Attendance Management** — Digital roll call with real-time parent SMS/WhatsApp notifications on arrival or absence
- **Behavioral & Disciplinary Records** — Log and track student conduct with automated reporting to parents and school management
- **Class Promotion Engine** — Automatically promote or flag students based on end-of-year results and pass thresholds
- **Bulk Student Import** — Upload student rosters via CSV or Excel for fast onboarding at the start of each academic year

### **Teacher Management**
- **Teacher Profiles & Assignments** — Manage teacher records, subject assignments, class allocations, and timetables
- **Workload Monitoring** — Track lesson delivery, assessment completion, and lesson plan submission per teacher
- **Performance Analytics** — Monitor class-level outcomes per teacher to identify support needs or high performers
- **Professional Development Logs** — Record and track training, certifications, and capacity-building activities
- **Lesson Delivery Tracking** — Confirm whether planned lessons were taught, rescheduled, or missed

### **Examination & Results Management**
- **Exam Scheduling** — Plan and publish examination timetables accessible to students, teachers, and parents
- **Score Entry Portal** — Teachers enter scores digitally; system validates, flags outliers, and prevents errors
- **Automated Report Cards** — Generate fully formatted, school-branded report cards for every student at the end of each term
- **Result Broadsheet** — Produce class-wide result broadsheets showing all students, subjects, scores, grades, and rankings
- **Principal's Comments Automation** — Suggest contextual comments for report cards based on student performance patterns
- **Result Portal for Parents** — Parents access their child's results securely via a parent portal or SMS

### **Parent & Communication Management**
- **Parent Portal** — Parents view attendance records, results, report cards, fee statements, and school announcements
- **WhatsApp Integration** — Send report cards, fee reminders, and school notices directly via WhatsApp
- **SMS Notifications** — Deliver attendance alerts, examination schedules, and important updates via SMS
- **Email Communication** — Send bulk or individual emails to parents, students, or staff
- **Announcement Board** — Publish school-wide or class-specific announcements visible on the portal

### **School Fees & Financial Management**
- **Fee Schedule Setup** — Define term fees, levies, and optional charges per class level
- **Installment Tracking** — Track partial payments and outstanding balances per student — critical for Nigerian private school operations
- **Payment Integration** — Accept payments via Paystack, Flutterwave, bank transfer, and cash; automatically reconcile records
- **Fee Defaulter Alerts** — Automatically flag and notify parents of outstanding payments before result release
- **Financial Reports** — Generate daily collection summaries, term revenue reports, and outstanding debt schedules
- **Receipt Generation** — Produce and share professional fee receipts instantly via WhatsApp, email, or print

### **Timetable & Academic Calendar**
- **Automated Timetable Generation** — Generate clash-free weekly timetables based on teacher availability, subjects, and classrooms
- **Academic Calendar Builder** — Set term dates, holiday periods, examination weeks, and events across the academic year
- **Calendar Sharing** — Publish the academic calendar to parents and students via the portal

### **Analytics & Reporting**
- **School Dashboard** — Visual overview of enrollment, attendance, fee collection, and academic performance across the school
- **Subject Performance Reports** — Identify subjects where students consistently underperform for targeted intervention
- **Student Progress Tracking** — Monitor individual student growth across terms and flag learning difficulties early
- **Teacher Effectiveness Reports** — Correlate teacher lesson delivery data with student outcomes
- **Government Compliance Reports** — Generate EMIS/NEMIS-compliant data reports for state ministries, UBEC, and SUBEB submissions
- **Examination Statistics** — Analyse class-wide pass rates, grade distributions, and improvement trends term-over-term

### **Library Management**
- **Book Catalog** — Maintain a digital catalog of all library resources by subject, author, and class level
- **Borrowing & Return Tracking** — Log book loans and returns with due date reminders sent to students
- **Overdue Alerts** — Automatically notify students and parents of overdue library materials

### **Subscription & Scalability**
- **Flexible Plans** — Free, Basic, Standard, and Professional tiers sized for nursery/primary schools up to large secondary schools
- **Scalable User Accounts** — Add teachers, admin staff, and parents as the school grows
- **Multi-School Support** — Group school owners manage multiple campuses from a single dashboard
- **Grace Period Management** — Continue operations during subscription renewal grace periods

### **Offline & Infrastructure Resilience**
- **Offline-First Design** — All critical functions (attendance, grading, lesson plan access) work without internet connectivity
- **Automatic Cloud Sync** — Data syncs to the cloud automatically when connectivity is restored
- **Low-Bandwidth Optimization** — Content compressed for operation on slow 3G networks
- **SMS Fallback** — Key notifications delivered via SMS when app connectivity is unavailable

### **Security & Access Control**
- **Role-Based Permissions** — Granular access control for Principals, Vice Principals, Teachers, Bursar, and Admin Staff
- **Secure Authentication** — JWT-based authentication with password encryption
- **Activity Audit Logs** — Full trail of every action taken by every user across the platform
- **Data Privacy Controls** — Student and parent data handled in compliance with NDPR (Nigeria Data Protection Regulation)
- **Session Management** — Automatic session timeout and secure password recovery

## 🚀 Available Scripts

In the project directory, you can run:

### `npm run dev` — in the root folder
Runs the full application (backend + frontend) in development mode with hot reload.

### `npm start` — in the client folder
Runs only the React frontend.
Open [http://localhost:3000](http://localhost:3000) to view it in your browser.

### `npm run seed` — in the root folder
Seeds the database with sample Nigerian school data including subjects, class levels, and a sample NERDC-aligned question bank.

### `npm run generate:timetable`
Runs the automated timetable generation engine for a school's current term.

### `npm run sync:offline`
Manually triggers offline data synchronization for testing purposes.

## 🛠 Technology Stack

**Frontend:** React, Redux Toolkit, React Router, Ant Design, Recharts, Moment.js, Sonner (Toast Notifications)

**Backend:** Node.js, Express.js, MongoDB, Mongoose

**AI & NLP:** OpenAI API / Claude API (Lesson Plan & Question Generation, Essay Grading), Custom NLP models for NERDC curriculum mapping

**Cloud Services:** AWS S3 (Document & Report Storage), Cloudinary (Image Management)

**Authentication:** JWT (JSON Web Tokens), NDPR-compliant data handling

**Payments:** Paystack, Flutterwave

**Communication:** Nodemailer (Email), Termii / Africa's Talking (SMS), WhatsApp Business API

**Testing:** Jest, React Testing Library, Supertest

## 🌍 Nigerian-Specific Design Considerations

EduCore AI is not a generic international platform adapted for Nigeria — it is built from the ground up for the Nigerian context:

- **Offline-first architecture** handles frequent power outages and unreliable internet without data loss
- **Low-bandwidth optimization** ensures the platform works on slow 3G/4G connections common in many Nigerian states
- **NERDC curriculum data** is pre-loaded and mapped to every subject generation module
- **Local payment systems** (Paystack, Flutterwave, bank transfer) are natively integrated
- **WhatsApp-first communication** reflects how Nigerian parents and teachers actually communicate
- **Installment fee tracking** handles the reality that many Nigerian families pay school fees in multiple tranches
- **WAEC/NECO dual-exam result management** addresses a uniquely Nigerian secondary school complexity
- **State ministry compliance** reports are formatted to match EMIS/NEMIS requirements of Nigerian state and federal education agencies
- **Pidgin-friendly UI language** options available for broader teacher adoption across literacy levels
